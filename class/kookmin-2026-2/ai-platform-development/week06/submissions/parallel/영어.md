# Department AI Practice Meeting

## Summary

The department is hosting a hands-on practice meeting where you can try using AI together with fellow students. The capacity is 12 people, and students who are new to AI practice are welcome to join. This English notice (along with the Japanese one) is provided only to help you understand the meeting information; the guidance and the practice session on the day of the meeting are all conducted in Korean. The meeting takes place on October 16, 2026, from 18:30 to 20:00 Korea time, in the Student Union Building meeting room, and there is no fee to attend. Please bring your own laptop and charger. Wi-Fi is available at the venue. To take part, leave your name in the department group chat by 18:00 on October 14, 2026 (Korea time); there is no separate application link.
